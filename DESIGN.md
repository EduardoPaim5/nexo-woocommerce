# Nexo visual direction

Frontend-design leads the initial design; Impeccable checks the finished implementation; stop-slop informs Portuguese interface copy; web-design-expert preserves native WooCommerce behavior.

The storefront resembles a considered industrial-design catalogue. Product photography and real shopping controls lead. Avoid gaming neon, decorative dashboard cards, fake testimonials and generic SaaS statistics.

Palette: paper #FAFAF7, ink #192924, pine #244C3D, pale sage #E6EDE6, lilac #E9E5F0, divider #D5DDD6. Warm yellow #E8D782 is reserved for the featured product backdrop. Body text never uses low contrast on colored panels.

Type: self-hosted Manrope, with strong compact display headings and restrained readable body copy. Display line height 1.04; body 1.65. Space scale 4,8,12,16,24,32,48,64,96. Header maximum width 1280; body 1200. Headlines and controls left aligned.

Composition: compact navigation and usable search; split first view with a large editorial statement and headphone photography; horizontal category navigation; clear four-column desktop product catalogue; quiet supporting split section; compact footer. Mobile uses two product columns, stacks the hero and keeps primary shopping controls reachable. Product cards exist to compare purchasable items, not as generic page scaffolding.

Signature: oversized lowercase nexo wordmark and two offset interlocking rounded squares, representing connection. The mark recurs in the hero, product notes, favicon and footer; shopping steps carry the same quiet green styling. Product-focused imagery is the memorable element. No scroll-triggered reveal animations. Functional state transitions respect reduced motion.

Refinement: product imagery is an original AI-generated conceptual studio collection on light gray #eeefeb, with upper-left diffused lighting and no manufacturer logos. Lifestyle editorial photography remains separate. Product captions describe daily uses; descriptions distinguish each concept without unverified battery or performance claims. The existing layout, Manrope and pine palette remain authoritative.

Review before implementation: a generic technology-store treatment would use black/neon and many promotional badges. This design uses a light catalogue, green functional controls and a single photographic focal point. Pages inherit WooCommerce notices, forms and validation.
