## Direção compartilhada / prompt

Create one square premium ecommerce product photograph for fictional Nexo desk accessories brand. Subject: [subject]. A single isolated item, no other props, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark. Consistent art direction: warm very light gray seamless studio background #eeefeb, soft diffused light from upper left, gentle realistic contact shadow, three-quarter view, centered with generous 18 percent breathing room on all sides, restrained industrial design, tactile realistic materials, sophisticated catalogue photography. This is a standalone product image, not a collage.
